# corporate-speak

Plain English is a liability in writing that will be forwarded. This skill
converts a clear message into one that is unambiguously professional and
substantially harder to quote against you later.

## Intensity scale

| Level | Register | Test |
|---|---|---|
| 1-3 | Polite human | A colleague would not notice anything |
| 4-6 | Calendar-invite English | "circle back", "bandwidth", "align" |
| 7-9 | All-hands English | Nouns become verbs. Verbs become journeys. |
| 10 | Leadership offsite | No sentence has a subject who could be blamed |
| 11 | Terminal | Grammatically valid. Contains no recoverable information. |

## Rules

1. **Never state a reason.** State a *context*. Reasons can be disagreed with;
   contexts can only be acknowledged.
2. **Delete the actor.** "I am not coming" becomes "attendance will not be
   possible". Nobody decided anything; it simply became the case.
3. **Promote every noun.** A conflict is a *scheduling conflict*, then a
   *prioritisation conflict*, then a *capacity-alignment consideration*.
4. **Convert refusal into enthusiasm.** The message must read as though the
   sender is thrilled about the thing they are declining to attend.
5. **Add one forward-looking commitment that cannot be measured.** "I will
   ensure the learnings are carried forward."
6. **Length scales with intensity.** At 11, the message is at least three times
   the length of the input and says strictly less.
7. **Never lie.** This is the constraint that makes it work: every clause must
   remain literally true. Corporate register is not deception, it is deniable
   compression. If a sentence would be false, make it vaguer, not falser.

## Vocabulary

- do → action, operationalise
- talk about → socialise, align on
- problem → challenge, headwind, opportunity area
- late → tracking behind plan
- no → not at this time
- I forgot → it fell outside the current prioritisation window
- I already know this → my current proficiency baseline is well established
- I am busy → my bandwidth is fully committed against Q3 deliverables

## Output

Return only the rewritten message. No preamble, no explanation, no note about
what you changed. The recipient must never see the seams.
